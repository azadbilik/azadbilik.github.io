# Musiqi · Music

[azadbilik.org/musiqi](https://azadbilik.org/musiqi/): the music section as a player (until 2026-11-28 at `/musiqi/dinle/`, which now redirects here). Every musician of the music pages with something to play, by **period** (first recordings 1900–1920, Soviet era 1920–1991, independence), **genre** (mugham, tar and kamancha, ashiq, classical, estrada, pop, jazz) and **school** (a master and their students), plus the stage and symphonic works found on Apple Music. Artist pages with tracks, albums and Apple Music / Spotify / YouTube links; one player bar that plays a queue (an artist, a work, a whole period). az/ru/en; hash routes (`#/a/Q…`, `#/w/Q…`, `#/c/period/soviet`, `#/c/genre/jazz`, `#/c/school/Q…`, `#/works`, `#/search?q=`).

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page |
| `data/catalog.json` | What it reads (built by `scripts/listen-build.mjs`; long URL prefixes shortened to `~a/ ~i1/ ~m/ ~u/ ~c/`, expanded by `X()` in the page) |

## Sources

- **Apple Music** (`data/apple.json`, `data/.cache/apple-match.json`): up to 10 tracks per musician with a 30-second preview, streamed from Apple and linked to the track; albums with artwork, years being the edition's, not the recording's. Matching and its terms: «Listening links» below.
- **Works** (`scripts/apple-works.mjs` → `data/.cache/apple-works.json`): Apple files classical recordings under the performers, so each work of [Bəstəkarlar](bestekarlar/) is searched by title and by the composer's surname. A track is kept when every distinctive word of one of the work's titles (az, ru, en, or a catalogue title in `ALIAS`) stands in the track title, or the album is named for the work alone; and the composer is named in a title or credited as an artist (a one-word name such as Niyazi only as a credit). A track goes to the earliest work it fits. 13 of 60 works found, 51 tracks.
- **YouTube** (`scripts/youtube-search.mjs` → `data/.cache/youtube-search.json`): most Soviet-era recordings and opera productions are only on YouTube, mostly re-uploaded by individuals. 229 searches (every Soviet-era musician by az and ru name, every work by title and composer); only **official channels** are kept, listed with the reason in `CHANNELS` in `scripts/listen-build.mjs`, each checked on its own page: AzTV and Mədəniyyət TV, İctimai TV and Radio, ATV Music, MUSZON (RAST Production for ATV), the Opera and Ballet Theatre, the Philharmonic, the Musical Theatre, the Museum of Musical Culture, Gəncə Philharmonic, Azərbaycanfilm, the Heydar Aliyev Foundation, Nakhchivan's Ministry of Culture, Gosteleradiofond (the Soviet TV and radio archive: three verified channels), Fikrət Əmirov's official channel; plus YouTube's «Topic» channels, and an official artist channel only for that artist's own videos; on a musician's own channel (Topic, official artist channel, or `OWN`: Arslan Növrəsli's and Ali Arshi's unbadged channels, checked by their content) the channel names the musician, so a track title need not. Also added by hand: Transcordancias · Global Music Live (a concert series filming its own concerts: Kamran Kərimov with Sahib Paşazadə). Tried and left out: ARB TV (talk-show chatter and news), «Farid Mamedov» (an unknown uploader of Kamran Kərimov's concerts). `EXTRA_Q` in `youtube-search.mjs` holds the more precise searches («Kamran Kərimov nağara»). Left out by name: «Apollo», «Azerbaijani Culture», «Mugamat», «AGA Channel», «Səslər muzeyi» and the like (re-uploads). A video must name the musician (first name and surname; all words of a longer name), or a work's title together with its composer or its kind (opera, ballet…); namesakes are kept apart by hand (Bülbül ≠ Polad Bülbüloğlu; Müslüm Maqomayev the composer only with his operas; Səxavət Məmmədov the tar player, b. 1993, and the xanəndə of 1953–1991 each without the other's channel: `NOT_CH`; the xanəndə is on Apple as «Səxavət Məmmədov», `FORCE` in `apple.mjs`); `SKIP_V` drops single non-music videos on an own channel. They play inside the page (youtube-nocookie.com embed through the IFrame API, in a panel above the player bar, driven by the bar's controls); a recording whose title stands in an official video of the same musician gets a Song / Video switch. Result (first run): 61 musicians with 262 videos (13 of them only there: Qaya, Soltan Hacıbəyov, Əfrasiyab Bədəlbəyli…), 32 works with 86 videos (22 only there).
- **Free recordings**: the public-domain recordings of `data/recordings.json`, `bestekarlar/` and `estrada/` (Commons), played in full and marked «azad».
- **Mughams** (`#/mughams`, `#/m/<id>`): the seven dəstgahs of `data/mugham.json` with their şöbələr, as on the map. A recording or video of the catalogue is filed under a dəstgah when its title names the dəstgah, a şöbə only that dəstgah has, or a zərbi mugham `mugham.json` assigns to it (a piece spelt differently from its şöbə, Simayi-Şəms, files under the şöbə). Letters are compared exactly except the Latin spellings (sh, ch, kh, gh, ä) and ə ≈ a/e, ü ≈ u, ö ≈ o: the looser Apple-search folding read «Xəzər» as «Hasar» and «Leyli» as «Feili», and dropping ş read French «sur» as Şur. Left out: titles naming two of the seven, or a dəstgah outside them (Orta Mahur, Mahur-Hindi); şöbələr shared by two dəstgahs (Vilayəti, Üşşaq…); «Rəşt» (a song); estrada and pop. The symphonic mughams of the works list (Amirov's Şur, Gülüstan-Bayatı-Şiraz, Niyazi's Rast) file by the work's title. On the page, the şöbələr are chips that filter the list; those with recordings carry a count. Beyond the catalogue's ten tracks per musician, `scripts/apple-mugham.mjs` collects the full album track lists of mugham musicians whose song lookup hit Apple's limit (Malik Mansurov, Alim Qasımov) and searches each dəstgah and zərbi mugham by name (az, us, ru storefronts); `listen-build.mjs` sorts these with the same matcher plus: in «X: Y» titles (Malik Mansurov's sets, «Shur: Saranj») X must name one of the seven, so «Qatar: Ayaq» stays out, and Y may name any of its şöbələr, also in Persian transliteration: compared by consonant skeleton (vowels, y/w and the ezafe -e/-ye/-o dropped; kh/x/h, k/q/gh, j/c one sound each; on word boundaries; a skeleton under 3 letters must be the whole part), so «Hejaz» = Hicaz, «Shekast-e Fars» = Şikəsteyi-fars, «Manand-e Mokhalef» = Manənd-Müxalif, «Bayat-e Tork» = Bayatı-Türk, «Ozzal» = Üzzal, while «Manad-e Hesar» is not Yədi-hasar and «Zil Segah» is not Segah's base (zil only to zil); translated names count too: Persian words put into Azerbaijani in any order («Masnavi-ye Kuchak» = Kiçik məsnəvi; kuchak = kiçik, bozorg = böyük), or the distinctive last word of one şöbə only («Mokhalef» = Manənd-Müxalif); with the dəstgah known, the sections every mugham has tag where it lists them (Çahargah's Bərdaşt, Bayatı-Şiraz's Ayaq), and «Maye» or the dəstgah's own name as the part is its «Mayeyi-…»; by hand (`SOBE_ALIAS`): «Mobarqa» = Mürəbbiqə, only where the dəstgah lists it (Segah, and Rast and Şur, where `data/mugham.json` has it by hand with a note: Malik Mansurov's sets have it, the cited Wikipedia lists do not); an album counts only when named for the dəstgah alone; sections every mugham has (Mayə, Bərdaşt, Dəraməd, Ayaq, Rəng, Zil, Təsnif) never decide; Persian spellings Homayun, Bayat-e Shiraz, Shushtar are accepted; ashiqs are left out (their «Hüseyni», «Arazbarı» are ashiq melodies of the same name); a search hit by a performer outside the catalogue needs muğam / təsnif / zərbi in its title or album and is shown under the dəstgah. 83 recordings and videos carry a şöbə. So far: Rast 4 + 26 + 8 videos, Şur 13 + 40 + 5, Segah 14 + 47 + 2, Çahargah 5 + 32, Bayatı-Şiraz 4 + 37, Şüştər 4 + 18 + 1, Hümayun 1 + 20. Rebuild: `node scripts/apple-mugham.mjs` (about 7 minutes from scratch), then `node scripts/listen-build.mjs`.
- **Tree** (`#/tree?g=<genre>`, «Ağac» in the sidebar): the genres of `data/genres.json` on a time axis (a tradition column, then 1900–2000; lanes: folk and tradition, classical, estrada and pop, jazz), arrows from what each came from (dashed: an outside root, European classical music, American jazz). Every date is from the music pages' data (first recording 1902; the first opera 1908, musical comedy 1910, ballet 1939, symphonic work 1947 from the works' premieres) or the Wikipedia article each node cites (symphonic mugham 1948; jazz 1926 and the 1930s; jazz-mugham the 1960s; ethno-jazz the 1990s, the group Rast from 1993; estrada the mid-20th century; pop's 1970s). Picking a genre lights its arrows and opens its text, sources, play button, works, musicians and teacher → student lines: the links of Silsilə, Bəstəkarlar and Caz (174, `tree.links` in the catalogue, with names of the 98 masters the player has no page for in `tree.people`; `data/genres.json` → `links` adds sourced ones by hand, Alim Qasımov → Sevda Ələkbərzadə) among the genre's people, as nested lists, a master with no teacher among them heading each line; text-derived links dashed with *.
- **Search**: names in any of az/ru/en; from 4 letters also by sound (Cyrillic to Latin, vowels dropped, b/p, d/t, k/q/g, c/j/ç, x/h alike), so «alekperzade» finds Ələkbərzadə and «beybutov» Behbudov.
- **Period**: the years from 18 to death (or now) against the three spans; «first recordings» only for someone born by 1890 or with a free recording of the time.
- **Genre**: the person's roles on the music map (khanende, tar, ashik) and sub-pages (composer, jazz, estrada); `GENRE_FIX` by hand.
- **School**: teacher links of [Silsilə](silsile/), Bəstəkarlar and Caz; a master with at least two playable students (all generations below them), the twelve largest. Teachers from abroad (Shostakovich, Lyadov…) are not in the catalogue.

## Gaps

Most Soviet recordings (Melodiya) are not on Apple Music, or sit under an orchestra or an anthology. No reliable match for Bülbül, Cabbar Qaryağdıoğlu (their free gramophone recordings play here), Qaya, the State Estrada Orchestra, Müslüm Maqomayev Sr., Şəfiqə Axundova, Soltan Hacıbəyov.

Not reachable for a build: russian-records.com (78 rpm database; captcha), MusicBrainz (busy at the time), staroeradio.ru and sovmusic.ru (no answer from here); Deezer has the same releases as Apple Music.

Rebuild: `node scripts/apple-works.mjs` (about 10 minutes from scratch), `node scripts/youtube-search.mjs` (about 15 minutes; one request every 4 s), then `node scripts/listen-build.mjs`; cached runs take seconds.

## Listening links (Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube)

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `listen.js` | The block in a person's card: Apple Music and Spotify links, up to 3 30-second previews, the Apple Music recordings list (az/ru/en) |
| `data/apple.json` | Per Wikidata id: Apple Music artist (id, URL, how it was matched), up to 3 previews (`previewUrl` + the track's Apple Music page), albums (name, year, URL) |
| `data/spotify.json` | Wikidata P1902 (artist) and P2205 (album) ids; plain link-outs, no Spotify API |
| `data/youtube.json` | Wikidata P2397 channels (`scripts/youtube.mjs`) |
| `scripts/apple-wd.mjs`, `scripts/apple.mjs`, `scripts/apple-lib.mjs` | Build: Wikidata labels and ids, then the iTunes Search/Lookup API (`data/.cache/` holds the raw answers, git-ignored) |

- Source: the public iTunes Search API (`itunes.apple.com/search`, `/lookup`; no key; at most ~18 requests a minute). Terms followed: every preview links back to the track on Apple Music and carries the «Apple Music» mark; previews are streamed from Apple's `previewUrl`, never downloaded, cached or mirrored; only URLs are stored. Links are labelled plainly «Apple Music» and «Spotify».
- Matching: Wikidata P2850 where present (12 people, authoritative); otherwise the Apple artist's name must fit one of the person's labels (az/ru/en/aliases, transliteration-tolerant, every word must match; storefronts az, us, ru). `DROP` in `scripts/apple.mjs` lists the namesakes and doubtful matches with reasons (a living namesake for a person long dead, a feature credit only, one artist page claimed by two people); `FORCE` and `ALSO` hold hand-checked ids. A person not on Wikidata P2850 with no album and fewer than three tracks is dropped.
- Xanəndələr (2026-11-28): every xanəndə of the musicians list was searched on Apple by their az name (the Latin search had missed Səxavət Məmmədov, Hacıbaba Hüseynov, Qulu Əsgərov, Mələkxanım Əyyubova, Gülyaz Məmmədova, Fərqanə Qasımova's own page) and on YouTube by name and as «… xanəndə»; the Apple ids were checked by repertoire and dates and set in `FORCE` (a hand-checked id is kept however few its tracks: Yaqub Məmmədov and Zülfü Adıgözəlov are on Apple only on the anthology «Great Singers of the Republic of Azerbaijan»). «Sattar» on Apple is an Iranian pop singer, not Səttar; on YouTube Səttar is the painter Səttar Bəhlulzadə and «Bülbül» also a song, kept apart in `NOT`. 55 of the 88 xanəndələr are in the player; the rest (mostly before recording, or only in private re-uploads) have nothing official to play.
- Aşıqlar (2026-11-28): every aşıq of the recording era (died 1930 or later; an older master's name only finds others singing his poems) searched the same way, on YouTube also as «Aşıq …» or «… saz». Apple has most of them only on anthologies: Ocora's «Azerbaïdjan, Anthologie des Ashiq» (Fəzail Miskinli, Mahmud Məmmədov, Gülarə Azaflı, Əli Quliyev, Altay Məmmədov) and ORED's archive. Where the name finds only others on YouTube (the three older Aşıq Əlis get the living Aşıq Əli; Aşıq Murad Ulduz gets Ulduz Sönməz; Aşıq Novruz the holiday…), `NO_YT` in `listen-build.mjs` leaves YouTube out for that person. 15 of the 88 aşıqlar are in the player.
- Tar and kamança players (2026-11-28): `data/musicians.json` takes only Wikidata's occupation «tar player», and Wikidata has no occupation for kamança players, so `scripts/instrumentalists.mjs` adds everyone with the instrument (P1303) tar or kamancheh and a tie to Azerbaijan → `data/instrumentalists.json` (singers and composers who also play are left out unless their occupations name the instrument). Searched the same way, on YouTube also as «… tar» / «… kamança»; the Apple ids checked by hand are in `FORCE` (Habil Əliyev with both his pages, Ramiz Quliyev's «Dialogues with the Tar», Elxan Mansurov, İmamyar Həsənov…).
- Previews: tracks of the artist's own page (one per album first); for composers, whose works Apple files under the performers, tracks whose album or title carries the composer's surname («Karayev: Piano Works»).
- Recordings lists: Apple's albums by release date. The year is the release of the edition in the Apple Music catalogue, often a reissue of an old recording, not the year it was recorded; the pages say so. On the jazz timeline the Apple Music row shows releases up to 2012.
- These are separate from the public-domain recordings the pages play (Commons); nothing from Apple is called free.
- Rebuild: `node scripts/apple-wd.mjs`, then `node scripts/apple.mjs` (resumable, about an hour from scratch; cached runs take seconds).
