# Estrada

[azadbilik.org/musiqi/estrada](https://azadbilik.org/musiqi/estrada/): Soviet-era Azerbaijani popular music: 19 hand-chosen singers, the Gaya quartet, the state estrada orchestra and song composers (Behbudov, Bulbul, Magomayev, Alakbarova, Khanlarova, Bulbuloglu, Mammadov, Tofig Guliyev, Mirishli...), as a lifespan timeline and cards (genre, awards from Wikidata). az/ru/en; `../ui.js`, `../ui.css`.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `data/estrada.raw.json` | What the page reads: Wikidata labels, dates, birthplace, genre P136, awards P166, Wikipedia titles (`scripts/estrada.mjs`) |
| `data/recordings.json` | Playable recordings with licence and evidence |

## Rights

In Azerbaijan sound recordings before 1976 are public domain; the song itself lasts the composer's life + 70 years. Only one recording plays: Mashadi Mammad Farzaliyev, «Ashiran» (1912), Commons, public domain. Not played, with reasons: Commons «Şən Azərbaycan» (CC BY-SA 4.0, uploader is not the author, no permission), Magomayev «Мужская верность» («own work» claim for a protected song), Behbudov on Commons (date unknown), three more Farzaliyev recordings (composer unchecked), archive.org uploads (no usable licence). Nothing found free for Behbudov, Magomayev, Alakbarova, Khanlarova, Bulbuloglu, Gaya, Mammadov, Imanov, Karimova. Melodiya digitisations: none with a clear licence found.

Rebuild: `node scripts/estrada.mjs`. Data: Wikidata CC0.
