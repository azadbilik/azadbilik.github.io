# Şamaxı

[azadbilik.org/gezinti/samaxi](https://azadbilik.org/gezinti/samaxi/): 10 places in and above the town, and two walks.

Build with `node gezinti/scripts/build.mjs samaxi`; how the data is made is in [../README.md](../README.md).

The 1667, 1859 and 1902 earthquakes left little of Shirvan's old capital, so this map is small.

- **No outline:** no walls survive.
- **Names set by hand:** five places whose Wikidata labels are long («…qəbirüstü abidəsi», «Yeddigünbəz türbələr kompleksi»).
- **Three graves with no article:** Seyid Əzim's grave, the Şaxəndan mausoleum and Sabir's grave. They are on the map with our own text; the facts about the mausoleum come from the Şaxəndan cemetery's article.
- **Left out:**
  - Q12849466 «Shamakhi Fortress»: its point is in Baku, and its picture is a 1583 Ottoman miniature.
  - The history museum: its only point is the city's centroid.
  - District sites 5–15 km out (Kələxana türbələri, Pirsaat Baba, Pir Mərdəkan): they could make a later route by car.
- **The Gülüstan walk** is a 6.6 km hike along OSM roads and tracks.
