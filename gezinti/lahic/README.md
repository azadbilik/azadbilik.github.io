# Lahıc

[azadbilik.org/gezinti/lahic](https://azadbilik.org/gezinti/lahic/): 9 places in the coppersmiths' village and one walk.

Build with `node gezinti/scripts/build.mjs lahic`; how the data is made is in [../README.md](../README.md).

- **Few sources:** Wikidata has only a few items here, and six of the nine places have no Wikipedia article. Their texts say so and stay to what their names and the village's article support. Each one is a place where an article could be written.
- **The museum and the Ağolu mosque are one place.** The museum is in the 1914 building known as the Ağaoğlu mosque, so the Ağolu mosque item (Q140662098) is not shown separately.
- **Not on the map:** the Girdiman (Niyal) fortress. The village article mentions it, but there is no item with a position.
