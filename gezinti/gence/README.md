# Gəncə · Ganja

[azadbilik.org/gezinti/gence](https://azadbilik.org/gezinti/gence/): 29 places in the old centre and three walks.

The underlay is the 1873 plan from [/atlas/baki](../../atlas/baki/).

Build with `node gezinti/scripts/build.mjs gence`; how the data is made is in [../README.md](../README.md).

- **No outline:** only fragments of the fortress walls survive, in Xan bağı and along the river.
- **Uğurlu xan karvansarası (Q12847060):** left off the map. Its Wikidata point is the city's own centroid, and OSM has no feature for it; the Russian article puts it on Əlibəy Hüseynzadə street.
- **Kiril-Mefodi kilsəsi:** the articles disagree on what it was built as (Lutheran, 1885, ru; Orthodox, 1902, en). The text says so.
- **Cavad xan dəftərxanası:** the original building was demolished in 2012, and the text says so.
- **Not on the map:**
  - the Armenian churches, whose attribution the articles dispute;
  - the destroyed khans' palace;
  - Nizami's mausoleum and İmamzadə, 6 km and more from the centre, which are for a later walk.
- **Qazaxlar məscidi and Mir Cəlal's house-museum:** on the map but off the walks, 1.5 km west of the centre.
