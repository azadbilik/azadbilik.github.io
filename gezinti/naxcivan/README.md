# Naxçıvan

[azadbilik.org/gezinti/naxcivan](https://azadbilik.org/gezinti/naxcivan/): 16 places in the old town and two walks.

Build with `node gezinti/scripts/build.mjs naxcivan`; how the data is made is in [../README.md](../README.md).

- **No outline:** no walls survive. The Old Fortress (Köhnəqala) is a place on the map.
- **Nuh peyğəmbər türbəsi:** the az and en articles disagree on its past. The az article says it was found in excavations at Köhnəqala; the en article says a medieval Armenian church stood here until 1953. The text gives both, with their sources.
- **Atabəylər Cümə məscidi:** demolished in the 20th century. It is on the map as a site, and its text says it no longer exists.
- **Came məscidi:** sources date it either to the 18th century (with a restoration in 1894) or to 1894 (its construction). The text gives both.
- **Left out:**
  - the history museum (Q12843155): its point is rounded to 39.2, 45.4;
  - the Kazım Qarabəkir Paşa mosque: 1.3 km east, with no photo;
  - the Bayraq Meydanı museum.
- **St Gevorg (Q94615349) and St Astvatsatsin (Q116980317):** shown as church sites, by the user's decision (2026-11-28). The texts say the en/ru articles report them destroyed (1997–2005 and by March 2005).
