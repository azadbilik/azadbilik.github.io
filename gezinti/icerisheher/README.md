# İçərişəhər · The Old City of Baku

[azadbilik.org/gezinti/icerisheher](https://azadbilik.org/gezinti/icerisheher/): 57 monuments inside the walls and three walks.

The underlay is the 1806 plan from [/atlas/baki](../../atlas/baki/).

Build with `node gezinti/scripts/build.mjs icerisheher`; how the data is made is in [../README.md](../README.md).

- **Positions corrected by hand (`at`):** the palace bath, Keyqubad mosque, the khans' palace and the Small Caravanserai. Wikidata puts these on the point of the whole complex; the new positions are the centres of their OSM outlines.
- **Own texts:** all the walks' stops except Zəncirli ev, whose article says nothing yet.
- **Left out:** demolished churches (St Nicholas, St Bartholomew), the duplicate Çin/Cin mosque item, and İsa bəy Hacınski's house, which is outside the walls.
